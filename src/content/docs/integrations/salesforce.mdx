Bring your Salesforce org into Omniscient with one sign-in. Omniscient reads your records and copies them in. It never writes to Salesforce.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | **Settings**, then **Imports**, then **Salesforce**, then **Connect Salesforce**. Or from Salesforce's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations**. |
| **Who connects** | Owners and admins |
| **What comes over** | Accounts, contacts, leads, opportunity stages, opportunities and notes |
| **Direction** | Salesforce into Omniscient, one time per import |
| **How often** | When you click **Import** |
| **What it asks for** | Read access to your org's records, and offline access so a long import can finish |
| **Disconnect** | Salesforce's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations** |

## Import from Salesforce

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Imports**, and pick **Salesforce**.
1. Click **Connect Salesforce** and sign in with a user who can read every account, contact, lead and opportunity you want.
1. Approve access. The import starts.
1. When it says **Finished**, check your records.

</Steps>

## What comes over

| In Salesforce | In Omniscient |
| --- | --- |
| Accounts | Companies, matched by their website |
| Contacts | People, linked to their account |
| Leads | People, with their lead status, company, title and phone |
| Opportunity stages | Stages in one pipeline, with each stage's default probability |
| Opportunities | Opportunities with name, amount, close date, stage, won or lost, and owner |
| Notes | Notes on the matching record |

Opportunity owners are matched by email to people in your workspace.

## If it fails

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Salesforce did not return a refresh token. Allow offline access on the connected app. | Ask your Salesforce admin to allow offline access for the Omniscient connected app |
| Salesforce didn't say where the org lives. Connect it again. | Connect again |
| The import's sign-in is gone. Start it again. | Click **Connect Salesforce** again |
| An import is already running. Wait for it to finish. | Wait. One import runs at a time. |

Sandboxes connect the same way when your server is set to use the sandbox sign-in.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on Salesforce's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Imported records stay.
