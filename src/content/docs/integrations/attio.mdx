Copy your Attio workspace into Omniscient. Omniscient reads your records once per import and never writes to Attio.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | **Settings**, then **Imports**, then **Attio**, then **Connect Attio** |
| **Who connects** | Owners and admins |
| **What comes over** | Companies, people, the deal stages, deals and notes |
| **Direction** | Attio into Omniscient, one time per import |
| **How often** | When you click **Import** |
| **What it asks for** | Read access to records, notes and workspace members |
| **Disconnect** | Attio's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations** |

## Import from Attio

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Imports**, and pick **Attio**.
1. Click **Connect Attio**, sign in and approve access.
1. The import runs: companies, people, pipelines, opportunities, then notes.
1. When it says **Finished**, check your records.

</Steps>

## What comes over

| In Attio | In Omniscient |
| --- | --- |
| Companies | Companies, matched by their domain |
| People | People, linked to their company |
| Deal stages | Stages in one pipeline. A stage with a celebration turned on becomes a won stage, and a stage named like "Lost" or "Churned" becomes a lost one. |
| Deals | Opportunities with name, amount, close date, stage and owner |
| Notes | Notes on the matching record |

Deal owners are matched by email to workspace members. Attio lists stay in Attio.

## Without one-click sign-in

If Attio sign-in isn't set up on your server, paste an access token instead. Make one in Attio under **Workspace settings**, then **Developers**, with read access to records, notes and workspace members.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on Attio's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Imported records stay.
