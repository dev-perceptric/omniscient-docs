If your team already uses a notetaker, you don't need to connect it. Most notetakers email a summary after each call. Omniscient finds those emails in your inbox and puts the summary on the meeting.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | Nothing to connect. Connect your mailbox, and the summaries come with it. |
| **Who** | Each person, through their own mailbox |
| **Apps** | Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, tl;dv, Read.ai, Zoom AI Companion and Notes by Gemini |
| **Direction** | Into Omniscient |
| **How often** | With each mailbox sync, about every 5 minutes |
| **What it reads** | The summary email: title, time, length, people, summary, action items and the recording link |
| **Turn off** | Pick **No call notes** under **Which calls get notes** in **Settings**, then **Call notes** |

## How it works

1. Your notetaker sends its summary email after the call.
1. Omniscient's sync reads the email and sees which app sent it.
1. It finds the meeting the email is about, by time and by who was there.
1. The summary, the action items and the recording link show on the meeting.

Summary emails are looked for on their own, apart from your other mail, so a summary is read even when Gmail files it under Updates or Promotions, which other syncs skip. A new mailbox picks up summaries from as far back as your sync settings reach.

## Which calls get notes

The same setting as for Zoom, Meet and Teams: **Notes for external meetings only**, **Notes for all meetings** or **No call notes**. Find it in **Settings**, then **Call notes**.

## Connect a notetaker as an app

You can also connect Fireflies, Fathom, Grain, Otter, tl;dv, Read.ai, Avoma, Gong or Webex in [the app store](/integrations/app-store/). That lets Otto look up what was said on a call when you ask. Each person connects their own notetaker.

<Aside type="note">
Omniscient writes its own notes from Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams transcripts. A notetaker's summary and Omniscient's notes can both show on the same meeting.
</Aside>
