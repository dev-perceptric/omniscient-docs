Copy your Pipedrive account into Omniscient. Omniscient reads your records once per import and never writes to Pipedrive.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | **Settings**, then **Imports**, then **Pipedrive**, then **Connect Pipedrive** |
| **Who connects** | Owners and admins |
| **What comes over** | Organizations, people, pipelines and stages, deals and notes |
| **Direction** | Pipedrive into Omniscient, one time per import |
| **How often** | When you click **Import** |
| **What it asks for** | Your Pipedrive API key, typed on the sign-in page |
| **Disconnect** | Pipedrive's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations** |

## Import from Pipedrive

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Imports**, and pick **Pipedrive**.
1. Click **Connect Pipedrive**. The sign-in page asks for your Pipedrive API key. Find it in Pipedrive under **Settings**, then **Personal preferences**, then **API**.
1. The import runs: companies, people, pipelines, opportunities, then notes.
1. When it says **Finished**, check your records.

</Steps>

## What comes over

| In Pipedrive | In Omniscient |
| --- | --- |
| Organizations | Companies |
| People | People, linked to their organization |
| Pipelines and stages | Pipelines and stages, with each stage's probability |
| Deals, open, won and lost | Opportunities with name, value, expected close date, stage and owner |
| Notes | Notes on the matching record |

Deleted deals are skipped. Deal owners are matched by email to workspace members.

<Aside type="note">
Pipedrive organizations don't carry a website, so they come in without a domain. Add domains later, and Omniscient links new emails to the company. Merge any company your inbox already made from the same domain.
</Aside>

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on Pipedrive's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Imported records stay.
