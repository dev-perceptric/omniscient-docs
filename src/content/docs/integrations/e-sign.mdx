Keep sending agreements from the e-sign tool your team already uses. Connect it to Omniscient so Otto can answer "Has Harbor Bank signed yet?" without you opening another tab.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Tools** | DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, PandaDoc, Qwilr |
| **Connect** | The tool's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations**, then **Connect** |
| **Who connects** | Owners and admins, once for the team |
| **Direction** | Read only. Omniscient never writes to the tool. |
| **How often** | When you ask Otto |
| **What it reads** | Which documents were sent, viewed and signed |
| **Disconnect** | The tool's card, then **Disconnect** |

## Connect your e-sign tool

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**, and pick **E-sign** in the **Category** menu.
1. Open your tool's card and click **Connect**.
1. Sign in to the tool and approve. Dropbox Sign, PandaDoc and Qwilr ask for an API key on their own sign-in page.

</Steps>

## Ask Otto

Once connected, ask in **Ask Otto**:

- "Which agreements in PandaDoc are still waiting on a signature?"
- "When did Lumen Health sign in DocuSign?"
- "List the Qwilr quotes viewed this week."

Otto looks them up and answers with what the tool says.

## What Otto won't do

- Write, draft, rewrite or suggest the words of an agreement.
- Send, void or change a document in the tool.
- Fill in or make a template.

To pull facts out of a contract you already have, like its term, renewal date or notice period, use the **Pull contract terms** skill in Ask Otto. See [Skills and knowledge](/otto/skills-and-knowledge/).

<Aside type="note">
Omniscient has no contracts screen in the CRM. Signing and review pages your buyers already got from Omniscient keep working.
</Aside>
