Arrowsterr tracks how AI answers talk about your brand, and how people find your site. Connected to Omniscient, it logs what your contacts did on your site onto their records.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | From Arrowsterr's settings, then **Allow** on Omniscient's approve page |
| **Who connects** | Owners and admins of the Omniscient workspace |
| **Direction** | Both ways. Arrowsterr logs activity into Omniscient and reads records as you allow. |
| **How often** | As things happen |
| **What it can do** | Contacts, accounts and opportunities; log marketing activity |
| **Disconnect** | The disconnect icon on Arrowsterr's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations** |

## Connect Arrowsterr

<Steps>

1. In Arrowsterr, open its settings and pick Omniscient.
1. Arrowsterr sends you to Omniscient's approve page.
1. An owner or admin clicks **Allow**.
1. Back in Arrowsterr, the connection shows the Omniscient workspace's name.

</Steps>

Arrowsterr's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations**, shows **Connected** once it's done.

## What you see in Omniscient

Arrowsterr logs activity on people, like a page view or a piece of content read. Each shows in the **Marketing** card on the person's record, with its time and a link.

## Disconnect

Click the disconnect icon on Arrowsterr's card in **Settings**, then **Integrations**, and confirm. Its key is revoked and anything it logged stays.
