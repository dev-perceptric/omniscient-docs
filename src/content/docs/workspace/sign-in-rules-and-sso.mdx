Workspace-wide sign-in rules live in **Settings**, then **Sign-in**. Only owners and admins see this page. The rules apply to everyone in the workspace.

## Require two-step verification

Under **Two-step verification**, turn on **Require for everyone**. People who don't use two-step yet set it up the next time they sign in. See [Account and security](/workspace/account-and-security/#two-step-verification).

## Choose ways to sign in

Under **Ways to sign in**, pick what your workspace accepts: Google, Microsoft, Apple, **Email code**, **Password**, **Passkey** and **Company SSO**. A method that isn't ready for your workspace shows **Not set up**.

## Set session length

Under **Session length**, choose how long people stay signed in before they sign in again: **No limit**, **8 hours**, **1 day**, **7 days** or **30 days**.

## Connect single sign-on

You can connect Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace or any SAML app. First you prove you own your domain, then you connect your identity provider.

### Verify your domain

<Steps>

1. Under **Domains and single sign-on**, click **Add domain** and type your company's domain, like `acme.com`.
1. Copy the **TXT name** and **TXT value** with **Copy name** and **Copy value**.
1. Add a TXT record with that name and value at your domain's DNS provider.
1. Back in Omniscient, click **Verify**. The domain shows **Verified** once the record is found.

</Steps>

DNS changes can take a while to show up. If **Verify** doesn't work right away, try again later.

### Set up SSO

<Steps>

1. Next to your verified domain, click **Set up SSO**.
1. In your identity provider, add Omniscient as a SAML app. Copy the **Entity ID**, **ACS URL** and **Metadata URL** from the window into it.
1. Copy the **Metadata URL from your identity provider** and paste it into Omniscient.
1. Click **Connect**. The domain now shows an **SSO** tag.

</Steps>

To make everyone at that domain use SSO, turn on **Only single sign-on** for the domain. To stop using SSO, click **Turn off SSO**.

<Aside type="tip">
A verified domain also makes joining by domain safer. Once you verify a domain, people who sign up with that domain are offered only your workspace.
</Aside>

## Provision members with SCIM

With SCIM, Okta and Entra add and remove members for you.

<Steps>

1. Under **Provisioning (SCIM)**, click **Make key**. The key shows once, so copy it right away.
1. Copy the **Base URL** with **Copy URL**.
1. In Okta or Entra, set up SCIM provisioning with the base URL and the key.

</Steps>

Click **Revoke** next to a key to stop it.

## Invite links

Make a link anyone can use to join, with a role and an end date. See [Invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team/#share-an-invite-link).

## Audit log

The **Audit log** shows members' sign-ins and security changes. Click **Download CSV** for the full log with the time, person, event, method, device and country.
