Every person in a workspace has one of three roles. The role decides what they can change. It doesn't decide what they can see: what a person sees from someone's email and meetings follows that mailbox's sharing setting.

| Role | Who it's for | How many |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The person who made the workspace, or the one it was handed to | One per workspace |
| **Admin** | People who set up the workspace and manage the team | Any number |
| **Member** | Everyone else | Any number |

Owners and admins change roles in **Settings**, then **Members**. See [Members and roles](/workspace/members-and-roles/) for how.

## The grid

**Yes** means the role can do it. **Own** means only for things that person made. A dash means no.

## Settings pages each role sees

Members don't see four settings pages at all: **Sign-in**, **Brand**, **Capture** and **Imports**. Every other page shows to everyone, with the controls a member can't use left out or turned off.

| Settings page | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account, Security, Notifications, Experience | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| General, Members, Templates, Plans & billing | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Sign-in, Brand | Yes | Yes | — |
| Mail & calendar, Call notes, Chrome extension, Integrations | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Data model, Workflows, Trash | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Capture, Imports | Yes | Yes | — |

## What a member sees from others' email

Roles don't change this. Each mailbox's owner picks one of three settings in **Settings**, then **Mail & calendar**:

| Sharing | Teammates see |
| --- | --- |
| **Share everything** | Subjects, previews and what Otto found, with quotes |
| **Share who and when** | Who and when, and what Otto found without quotes. No subject or preview. |
| **Keep private** | Nothing. What Otto finds stays with the mailbox's owner. |

<Aside type="note">
When a member tries something only owners and admins can do, Omniscient says so, for example "Only owners and admins can invite people." or "Only owners and admins can manage the API." The [error guide](/reference/error-messages/#team-and-roles) lists them all.
</Aside>
