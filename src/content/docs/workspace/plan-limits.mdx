This page lists the plans, what they cost, and every limit the app holds you to. A value marked "check" couldn't be confirmed in the product itself. Ask us before you rely on it.

## Plans and prices

Prices are per seat per month, in US dollars.

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Billed yearly | $0 | $19 | $49 | $99 |
| Billed monthly | $0 | $23 | $59 | $119 |
| Yearly charge per seat | $0 | $228 | $588 | $1,188 |
| Free trial | | | 14 days, no card | |

Yearly plans are charged once a year. When you add or remove a Seller seat, Stripe charges or credits the difference for the rest of the period.

### What the app checks today

Most plan lines are not locked in the app yet. These are:

| Feature | Lowest plan | What happens on a lower plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kanban board on Companies, People and Opportunities | Pro | Picking **Kanban** opens **Plans & billing** |
| Free seats | Pro | The seat menu doesn't show. The database answers "Free seats come with Pro and Business". |
| Agents | Starter (2), Pro (5), Business (15) | Adding one more answers "Your plan includes 5 agents" |

The plan rules in the app's code also name Ask Otto, meetings and drafts as Starter features, and field rules and prep as Pro features. They are not locked yet: check.

## Credits

Finding leads and filling in contact details use credits. Each person has their own daily allowance. It resets each day.

| What | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Daily allowance per person | 1,000 (the server's default) |
| Each lead row Otto asks for | 1 |
| Each email or phone lookup, in the app, Otto or the Chrome extension | 10 |
| A person who already has an email | 0 |
| Lead plans Otto can make per person per day | 60 |

A lookup that fails gives its credits back. When the day's credits run out, you see "Today's enrichment allowance is unavailable or exhausted." Credits aren't tied to a plan: check with us before you plan around a higher allowance.

## Sign-in limits

These stop people from guessing passwords. Each counts per email address and per network address.

| Try | Per email | Per network address |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in emails | 5 an hour | 20 an hour |
| Password tries | 10 an hour | 30 an hour |
| Wrong codes | 5 in 15 minutes, then that code is done | 30 an hour |
| Reset emails | 3 an hour | 10 an hour |

Past a limit, you see a line like "Too many sign-in emails. Try again in 12 minutes."
