## Where to start

If your workspace is new, do these three in order: [connect the whole team](/recipes/connect-the-whole-team/), [import from another CRM](/recipes/import-from-another-crm/) and [set up a pipeline](/recipes/set-up-a-pipeline/). Then pick the rest as you need them.
