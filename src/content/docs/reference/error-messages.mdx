This page lists the messages Omniscient shows when something can't be done. Find the words you saw with your browser's search (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>). Names in *italics* stand for the real name, email or number in your message.

## How errors work in Omniscient

- **Hiccups retry on their own.** A timeout, a busy server or a dropped connection is tried again with growing waits. You don't see a message unless every try fails.
- **What's left shows as one line with Retry.** At the bottom of the screen you see a short line like "Couldn't save Amount" and a **Retry** button. Click it to try again.
- **Big jobs never stop for one bad row.** When you save, delete or import many records, a batch that fails is split so the rest still go through. The line then says how many couldn't be saved.
- **You never see raw database text.** Anything meant only for developers becomes "Something went wrong. Try again."

## Messages you can see anywhere

| Message | What it means | How to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Something went wrong. Try again. | An error Omniscient couldn't put into plain words | Click **Retry** or do it again. If it keeps happening, reload the page. |
| We couldn't reach the server. Check your connection and try again. | Your browser lost its connection | Check your Wi-Fi or VPN, then try again |
| Couldn't save *field* | A change didn't save after several tries | Click **Retry** on the line |
| Couldn't delete. Try again. | A delete didn't go through | Click **Retry** |
| Please sign in again. | Your session ended | Sign in again. You come back to the same page. |
| You are not in this workspace. | You were removed, or the link is for another workspace | Switch workspace from the menu, or ask an admin to invite you |
| *Page* couldn't load. The rest of Omniscient still works. | One screen broke. The rest of the app keeps working. | Click **Try again** or **Reload**. If it keeps happening, click **Copy details** and send them to support. |
