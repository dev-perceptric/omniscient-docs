The words you see in Omniscient, in alphabetical order.

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | Everything that happened with a record, in time order: emails, meetings, notes, call notes and field changes |
| Admin | A role that can set up the workspace: invite people, change the data model, connect team apps and manage billing |
| Assignment | The rule that picks who owns new opportunities from forms, webhooks and handoffs. Set in **Settings**, then **Capture**. |
| Brief | A short summary by Otto at the top of a record, with the facts he found |
| Call notes | Notes Otto writes from a call's transcript |
| Capture | Forms, inbound webhook addresses and assignment rules that bring new leads in |
| Company | An organization you work with. Made from a work email domain, by hand or by import. |
| Data model | The objects your CRM holds and the fields on each one |
| Digest | A short email each weekday morning with today's meetings, emails to answer and opportunities that need attention |
| Do not track | People and domains that never sync from your mailbox |
| Field | One piece of information on a record, like Stage or Title |
| Free seat | A no-cost seat on Pro and Business for teammates who only view records or log their own email and meetings |
| Go live | Make a workflow's draft the live version |
| Knowledge | Short documents about your business that Otto reads before every answer |
| Last touch | The last email or meeting with a record |
| Lifecycle | Where a person is with you: New, Contacted, Engaged, Qualified, Customer or Lost |
| MCP server | The address that lets AI apps like Claude and ChatGPT work with your CRM |
| Member | The standard role in a workspace |
| Needs reply | A thread where the customer wrote last and nobody has answered yet |
| Next step | A short line on an opportunity saying what happens next |
| Object | A kind of record, like companies or a custom object such as projects |
| Opportunity | A deal you are working on with a company, in a pipeline stage |
| Otto | The agent in Omniscient. He answers questions, suggests updates, drafts emails and runs skills. |
| Owner | The person who made the workspace. Also, the teammate who runs an opportunity. |
| Person | Someone you email or meet with outside your team, matched by email address |
| Pipeline | The stages an opportunity moves through, from first contact to won or lost |
| Prep brief | Otto's short write-up before a meeting with a customer |
| Record | One company, person, opportunity or custom object entry |
| Record updates | Field changes Otto suggests from calls and emails, each with its quote, for you to accept or skip |
| Run | One person's trip through a workflow |
| Seller seat | A paid seat on your plan |
| Sharing | What teammates see from your mailbox: everything, only who and when, or nothing |
| Skill | A written playbook Otto follows for one kind of job. System, Workspace or Personal. |
| Stage | One step in a pipeline, with a win chance |
| Team domain | Your company's email domain. People with an address on it count as teammates. |
| Template | A message your team wrote for LinkedIn or email, with fields like `{{first_name}}` |
| Trash | Where deleted records wait for 30 days before they're erased |
| View | A saved way of looking at a list: filters, sorts, layout and columns |
| Weighted | An opportunity's amount times its win chance |
| Win chance | How likely an opportunity in a stage is to be won, as a percent |
| Workflow | Steps that run on their own when something happens, like sending a follow-up after a meeting |
| Workspace | Your team's shared CRM, with its own members, settings and plan |
