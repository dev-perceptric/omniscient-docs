## Getting started

<details>
<summary>Do I have to leave my current CRM?</summary>

No. Connect your inbox and calendar and run Omniscient next to your current CRM. When you're ready, import your companies, people, opportunities and notes from HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio or Pipedrive. See [CRM imports](/integrations/crm-imports/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is my workspace empty?</summary>

New workspaces start empty, and Omniscient never adds sample records. Connect your inbox and your CRM fills in from your real conversations. See [Connect your inbox](/get-started/connect-your-inbox/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does the first sync take?</summary>

It depends on how far back you chose and how much mail you have. Records start showing within a few minutes and keep filling in while you work. You can follow its status in **Settings**, then **Mail & calendar**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which email providers work?</summary>

Google (Gmail and Google Workspace), Microsoft 365 and Outlook, and any other mailbox that supports IMAP, such as Zoho, Fastmail, iCloud, Yahoo, AOL, GoDaddy, Titan or Hostinger.

</details>

## Privacy and email

<details>
<summary>Who on my team can see my email?</summary>

You decide for your own mailbox: **Share everything**, **Share who and when** or **Keep private**. The rule is enforced in the database, so it holds everywhere, including in Otto's answers. See [Email](/email-and-calendar/email/#who-sees-your-email).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Omniscient send emails on its own?</summary>

No. It sends an email only when you click **Send**, or when a workflow whose emails someone approved sends them from the owner's mailbox. Otto's drafts wait for you to send them. Omniscient never deletes or edits anything in your mailbox.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is my data used to train AI models?</summary>

No. Your emails and calls are never used to train AI models, and Google's Limited Use rules apply to data from Google accounts.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I keep some people out of Omniscient?</summary>

Yes. Add their emails or domains to **Do not track** in your sync settings, like `lawyer@firm.com` or `family.com`. You can also choose to add people only when you reach out to them, and to leave out personal addresses like gmail.com.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does a bot join my calls?</summary>

No. Otto reads the transcripts Google Meet, Teams and Zoom already make, or a transcript or recording you add yourself. See [Meetings and call notes](/email-and-calendar/meetings-and-call-notes/).

</details>

## Otto

<details>
<summary>What if Otto gets something wrong?</summary>

Every value Otto suggests shows the quote it came from and waits for you to accept or skip it. Values a person typed are never changed without a click, and every change shows in the field's history.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can Otto write contracts or message templates?</summary>

No. Otto never writes, drafts or suggests contract text, and he never writes templates. He can read a contract and pull out its terms, each with its quote and page. Templates are always written by your team.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I make Otto's answers fit my business?</summary>

Add short pages to **Knowledge** about what you sell, your prices, your ideal customer and your competitors. Otto reads them before every answer. For jobs you repeat, make a skill. See [Skills and Knowledge](/otto/skills-and-knowledge/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is Think harder grayed out?</summary>

You've used today's **Think harder** answers. Click **Usage today** in the chat box to see what's left and when it resets. Regular answers keep working.

</details>

## LinkedIn and the Chrome extension

<details>
<summary>Does Omniscient post or send messages on LinkedIn?</summary>

Never. Omniscient doesn't send anything on LinkedIn and doesn't read LinkedIn in the background. The Chrome extension only saves what you're looking at when you click, and it puts a template in the message box for you to send yourself.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How many people can I save from a LinkedIn search?</summary>

Up to 10 a click. To save more, scroll or go to the next page on LinkedIn yourself, then save again. The extension never scrolls or pages for you.

</details>

## Plans and billing

<details>
<summary>How does the free trial work?</summary>

Every new workspace gets 14 days of Pro for free, with no card needed. When it ends, the workspace moves to Free and your data stays safe. Pick a plan any time in **Settings**, then **Plans & billing**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who gets a Free seat?</summary>

Teammates who only view records or log their own email and meetings. Free seats come with a workspace that has at least one Pro or Business seat.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the AI included in the price?</summary>

Yes. Otto is included in every paid seat, so a busy month never adds a usage bill.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do you offer discounts for bigger teams?</summary>

Yes. Teams of 21 to 50 seats get 10% off, and 51 to 100 seats get 20% off. For more than 100 seats, write to [hello@tryomniscient.com](mailto:hello@tryomniscient.com).

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel?</summary>

Owners and admins click **Manage billing** in **Settings**, then **Plans & billing**, and cancel in the billing portal.

</details>

## Your data

<details>
<summary>Can I get my data out?</summary>

Yes. Export any list as CSV, Excel or JSON from **Import / Export**. Developers can also use the [REST API](/developers/api/). See [Data and Trash](/workspace/data-and-trash/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens to my data if I disconnect my mailbox?</summary>

Omniscient stops syncing and deletes the emails and meetings that mailbox brought in. Your companies, people and opportunities stay.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?</summary>

Yes, for 30 days. Open **Settings**, then **Trash**, find it and click **Restore**. Right after a delete, you can also click **Undo** or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd>.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Omniscient have tasks or a Lists page?</summary>

No. Keep what comes next in an opportunity's **Next step** field, and use saved views to keep lists of records. See [Views](/crm/views/).

</details>
