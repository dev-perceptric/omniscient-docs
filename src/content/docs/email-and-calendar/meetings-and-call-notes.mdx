Your calendar syncs with your mailbox. Each meeting with people outside your team lands on their records, gets a prep brief before it starts, and gets notes from Otto after it ends.

## The Meetings page

Click **Meetings** under **Intelligence** in the sidebar. Switch between **Upcoming**, **Past** and **All**, and use **Search meetings** to find one. The list shows each meeting with when it is, how long it runs, the company, the opportunity and the attendees. A **Prep brief ready** tag shows on meetings Otto has prepared.

To track internal meetings too, set **Calendar** to **All meetings** in your [sync settings](/get-started/connect-your-inbox/#choose-what-syncs).

## A meeting's page

Click a meeting to open it. It has two tabs:

- **Prep brief** opens first for meetings that haven't happened yet.
- **Notes** opens first once the meeting is over.

The rail on the right shows the details and the people on the invite, linked to their records.

### Prep brief

Up to a day before a meeting with a customer, Otto writes a short brief: who is coming, where things stand, what is still open and what to aim for. Prep briefs are on Pro and Business.

For a fuller brief, or a brief for all of today's meetings, ask Otto "prep me for my meetings today". See [Skills and Knowledge](/otto/skills-and-knowledge/#system-skills).

### Notes and what we learned

After the call, the **Notes** tab shows Otto's notes, the next steps and the promises made. **What we learned** lists the facts Otto pulled out. **Record updates to review** lists changes Otto suggests for the company, person or opportunity, each with **Accept** and **Skip**. See [Record updates and drafts](/otto/record-updates-and-drafts/).

## Turn on call notes

Otto writes notes from the transcripts your meeting tools already make. Nothing joins your calls.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Call notes**.
1. For Google accounts, click **Turn on** next to **Google Meet**. For Microsoft accounts, click **Turn on** next to **Microsoft Teams**. Approve the extra access.
1. For Zoom, click **Connect Zoom** and sign in. Add more Zoom accounts the same way.

</Steps>

Then pick which calls get notes under **Which calls get notes**:

| Option | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Notes for external meetings only | Calls with people outside your company. This is the suggested choice. |
| Notes for all meetings | Every call you're in, including internal ones |
| No call notes | None. You can still add notes to any meeting from its page. |

Click **Save** when you are done.

<Aside type="tip">
Transcripts have to be on in your meeting tool. In Google Meet, Teams and Zoom, turn on transcription or recording for your calls so there's a transcript for Otto to read.
</Aside>

### Notes from notetaker apps

If you use a notetaker like Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, tl;dv, Read.ai, Zoom AI Companion or Gemini, its summary emails in your inbox are matched to the meeting and show on it too.

## Add notes yourself

For a past meeting without notes, open it and click **Add notes**. The **Add call notes** window has three ways in:

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Paste text">
Paste the transcript into the box.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Transcript file">
Choose a .txt, .vtt or .srt file. Zoom, Meet, Teams and most note takers export these.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Recording">
Choose an audio or video file up to 500 MB. Omniscient turns the speech into text, then deletes the audio.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

Otto writes the notes and pulls out next steps, promises and deal details. The transcript itself is not stored.
