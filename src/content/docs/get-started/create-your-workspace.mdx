Your first sign-in creates your account and a workspace for your team. A short setup then builds a CRM that fits your business. New workspaces start on a 14-day free trial of Pro, and no card is needed.

## Create your account

<Steps>

1. **Open the sign-up page**

   Go to [app.tryomniscient.com/signup](https://app.tryomniscient.com/signup). The page says **Create your account**.

1. **Pick how you sign in**

   Click **Continue with Google**, or type your **Work email** and click **Continue with email**. With email, Omniscient sends you a link and a 6-digit code. Open the link, or type the code in the boxes. You can also choose **Sign up with a password**.

1. **Join your team, or start fresh**

   If people at your company already use Omniscient, you see **Join your team** with their workspace. Click **Join** next to it. To start your own, click **Create a new workspace**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Use your work email. Omniscient uses its domain to tell your teammates apart from the people you sell to.
</Aside>

## Set up your CRM

The first time you open a new workspace, Otto walks you through setup. Owners and admins see every step. Teammates who join later only connect their inbox.

<Steps>

1. **Let's build your CRM**

   Type your **Company website** and **Company name**, then click **Start**. Otto reads your site.

1. **Your CRM, for your business**

   Otto picks an industry and shows the pipelines, fields and words it will set up. Turn on **Tailor with Otto** to add your own services and words from your site. Click **Set up my CRM**, or **Skip for now** to keep the standard setup.

1. **Connect your inbox**

   Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**, or **Use another email** for any other mailbox. See [Connect your inbox](/get-started/connect-your-inbox/).

1. **Watch it fill in**

   Otto turns your recent email and meetings into companies, people and opportunities while you watch. Click **Continue** when you are ready. Otto keeps going in the background.

1. **Bring your team**

   Add the work emails of the people you want in the workspace. The button then reads **Invite 2 and finish** (with your own count). Click it, or click **Skip for now**. See [Invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team/).

1. **Open Omniscient**

   The last screen shows what Otto built and a short **Start here** list. Click **Open Omniscient** to go to your CRM.

</Steps>

## Industries Otto can set up

| Industry | First pipeline and its stages |
| --- | --- |
| Software and SaaS | New business: Lead, Discovery, Demo, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost |
| Agency and consulting | Sales: Inquiry, Discovery call, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Signed, Lost |
| Real estate | Buyers: New lead, Contacted, Showing, Offer, Under contract, Closed, Lost |
| Home services | Jobs: Inquiry, Estimate scheduled, Estimate sent, Booked, Done, Paid, Lost |
| Recruiting | Submissions: Sourced, Screened, Submitted, Interview, Offer, Placed, Rejected |
| Financial advice | New business: Prospect, Discovery meeting, Plan presented, Paperwork, Funded, Not now |
| Health and wellness clinics | New patients: Inquiry, Consult booked, Consult done, Plan accepted, Not going ahead |
| Construction | Bids: Invited, Estimating, Bid submitted, Shortlisted, Awarded, Lost |
| Nonprofit | Major gifts and grants: Identified, Cultivating, Ask planned, Asked, Pledged, Received, Declined |
| Legal | Intake: Inquiry, Conflict check, Consult, Engagement sent, Engaged, Declined |

You can change any of it later. Admins can run the setup again from **Settings**, then **General**, then **Industry**, with **Set up an industry**.

## Your workspace starts empty

Omniscient never adds sample records. Everything you see comes from your own inbox, your imports or what you add yourself.
