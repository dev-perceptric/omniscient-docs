Omniscient does most of the setup from your inbox. These steps help you check its work and get the most out of Otto in your first days. Each one takes a few minutes.

## Day one

<Steps>

1. **Check your opportunities**

   Open **Opportunities**. Otto set their stages from your emails and meetings. Fix any stage that looks wrong, and drag cards on the board if you prefer. [Opportunities and pipelines](/crm/opportunities-and-pipelines/)

1. **Ask Otto what needs you today**

   Open **Ask Otto** and ask "What needs my attention today?" Otto looks at replies waiting, opportunities to move and meetings to prep. [Ask Otto](/otto/ask-otto/)

1. **Turn on call notes**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Call notes**. Turn on Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, or click **Connect Zoom**. Otto then writes notes after your calls. [Meetings and call notes](/email-and-calendar/meetings-and-call-notes/)

</Steps>

## Days two and three

<Steps>

1. **Tell Otto about your business**

   Open **Knowledge** and click **New document**. Write down what you sell, your prices, who you sell to and how you like to sound. Otto reads it before every answer. [Skills and Knowledge](/otto/skills-and-knowledge/)

1. **Invite your team**

   A CRM works better when everyone's conversations are in it. [Invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team/)

1. **Save a view you will use every day**

   Filter a list the way you work, like your own open opportunities, then click **Save as view**. [Views](/crm/views/)

</Steps>

## Later in the week

<Steps>

1. **Set up a follow-up workflow**

   Ask Otto to "build a workflow that follows up two days after a meeting". Otto saves it as a draft for you to check, approve and take live. [Workflows](/otto/workflows/)

1. **Add the Chrome extension**

   Save people from LinkedIn with a click and see their record next to Gmail. [Chrome extension](/chrome-extension/install-and-connect/)

1. **Read your morning digest**

   Each weekday morning you get a short email with today's meetings, emails to answer and opportunities that need attention. Turn it on or off in **Settings**, then **Notifications**. [Account and security](/workspace/account-and-security/#notifications)

</Steps>

## Getting-started emails

In your first weeks, Omniscient sends the person who made the workspace a few short emails, each with one next step. An email is skipped once you have done its step. Turn them off in **Settings**, then **Notifications**, with **Getting-started tips**, or with the link in any of the emails.
