Everyone on the team shares the CRM. Each person connects their own inbox and picks their own sync settings, so the CRM gets fuller with every teammate who joins. Owners and admins can invite people.

## Invite by email

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Members**.
1. Type a work email in **teammate@company.com**.
1. Pick a role: **Member** or **Admin**. See [Members and roles](/workspace/members-and-roles/).
1. Click **Invite**.

</Steps>

The person gets an email and joins when they sign in with that address. Until then they show with an **Invite sent** tag. Click **Send again** to resend it, or **Withdraw invite** to cancel it.

## Let your company domain join

When your workspace has a team domain, **Members** shows a switch: **Anyone with an @yourcompany.com email can join**. Turn it on, and teammates who sign up with that domain see **Join your team** with your workspace.

Your team domain shows in **Settings**, then **General**. People on that domain count as teammates, so their emails never turn into customer records.

## Share an invite link

Admins can make a link anyone can use to join.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Sign-in**, then **Invite links**.
1. Pick the role new people get, **Member** or **Admin**.
1. Pick when the link expires: **1 day**, **7 days** or **30 days**.
1. Click **Make link**. **Your invite link** shows once, so copy it right away.

</Steps>

Each link in the list shows how many people joined with it and when it expires. To stop a link early, click **Turn off** next to it.

<Aside type="caution">
Anyone with the link can join until it expires. Share it only in places your team controls.
</Aside>

## What a new teammate does

1. Signs in with the invited email.
1. Connects their inbox in the short setup. See [Connect your inbox](/get-started/connect-your-inbox/).
1. Picks what their teammates see from their mailbox.

## Seats

On Pro and Business, admins can switch a teammate from a **Seller seat** to a **Free seat**. Free seats suit people who only view records or log their own email and meetings. See [Plans and billing](/workspace/plans-and-billing/).
