Open any row on a list to see its record. A record page has a header with the name, a short summary of what's next, tabs for its history and a rail of fields on the right.

## Side panel or full page

From a list, a record can open in a side panel next to the list or as a full page. Open the record's menu to switch: **Always open full page** or **Open in side panel next time**. Admins can set the default for each object in the [data model](/crm/data-model/), under **Layout**, then **Navigation**.

## What's next

Under the name, a record shows the parts it has:

- The open opportunity and its stage.
- **Brief**, a short summary tagged with Otto's name, plus the facts he found in your emails and calls.
- The next meeting on the calendar.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | Everything in time order: emails, meetings, call notes, notes and field changes |
| Emails | Email threads with this record. Click one to read the whole thread. |
| Meetings | Meetings coming up and past, each with its date |
| Notes | Notes your team wrote, and call notes from Otto |
| Files | Files added to the record. Drop files on the tab or click to upload, up to 25 MB each. |

Each tab is its own address, so you can share a link straight to a record's emails or notes.

### Field history

Every field change shows on the **Activity** tab with who made it: **You**, **Otto** or **Sync**. You can see what a field was before and what it is now.

## Fields

The rail on the right shows the record's fields in groups. Click a value to change it. The **More** group holds the rest of the fields, and **System** shows when the record was created and its last touch. Admins can change the groups in the [data model](/crm/data-model/).

To add a field right from the record, open **More** and add it there.

People also show a **Marketing** card when a connected tool, such as Claritia or Arrowsterr, logs activity like a form fill or an email click.

## Write a note

Open the **Notes** tab, type in **Write a note** and click **Add note**.

You can also see and add notes across the whole CRM from **Notes** in the sidebar. Click **New note**, link it to a company, opportunity or person, and write the note.

## Email from a record

Click **New email** at the top of the **Emails** tab to write to this person or company from your own mailbox. See [Email](/email-and-calendar/email/) for sending and replies.

## Record menu

The **⋯** menu on a record holds:

- **Merge a duplicate** (companies and people)
- **Copy link**
- **Always open full page** or **Open in side panel next time**
- **Delete**

<Aside type="tip">
Ask Otto about the record you are looking at. The Otto box at the bottom of the page knows which record is open, so "what did we agree last time?" just works.
</Aside>
